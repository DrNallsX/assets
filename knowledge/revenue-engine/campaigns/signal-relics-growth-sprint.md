# Signal Relics Growth Sprint

## Objective
Create repeated qualified traffic to Ko-fi, measure real conversion through Stripe, and expand average order value using a four-product ladder before spending materially on NFT gas or paid promotion.

## Offer ladder
1. Mobile Vault — $7+ entry product.
2. Original 24 — $12+ core product.
3. Motion Vault — $18+ premium visual product.
4. Complete Vault — $29+ best-value bundle.
5. Desktop & Stream Pack — $9+ cross-sell.

## Traffic priority
1. Pinterest: evergreen visual-search traffic. Publish six distinct vertical pins linked to the Ko-fi product/store and organize them into descriptive boards.
2. TikTok/Reels: short-form discovery. Lead with curiosity and visual transformation, not a hard sales pitch. Use original short videos; disclose computer-generated art accurately.
3. Instagram: portrait posts/reels and profile link to Ko-fi.
4. Ko-fi native: animated GIF preview, posts, shop categories, social links, and default Shop tab.
5. Facebook/X: keep live launch posts pinned/featured when possible and use follow-up posts with individual relics rather than reposting identical copy.

## Creative rules
- First-frame hook within one second.
- Show the art before explaining it.
- Use curiosity prompts: “Which signal is yours?” / “You found a signal.”
- Avoid fake scarcity, fake sales, fabricated testimonials, engagement bait, or guaranteed income/value claims.
- Every creative must identify the work as computer-generated/deterministic where the platform or context requires disclosure.

## Measurement
Primary metric: confirmed Stripe revenue.
Secondary: product mix and average order value.
Do not optimize to likes alone.

## NFT gate
Proceed with OpenSea/Base Genesis Eight setup, but do not scale minting or spend materially until at least one of these occurs:
- confirmed digital-product sales;
- repeated inbound collector interest;
- measurable sustained traffic indicating collector demand.

OpenSea target: Open Collection, Base, ERC-1155, eight 1/1 items, suggested 5% creator earnings if enforcement is available.
Rarible: secondary only; do not pay a 1,000 RARI collection-listing charge without demonstrated ROI.

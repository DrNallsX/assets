# Signal Relics — Initial Launch Plan

## Objective

Use one body of original work to create several legitimate products without duplicating ownership claims or misleading buyers. NFT/token ownership and downloadable-file licenses must be described separately.

## Release inventory

- 24 unique Signal Relic SVG originals for the pilot collection.
- 1 phone-wallpaper pack derived from selected originals.
- 1 desktop-wallpaper pack.
- 1 printable-art starter pack.
- 1 creator/design asset bundle.
- 1 custom generative commission offer.

## Channel ladder

### 1. Zora

Post a small series of strongest originals first. Each post is treated as a creator-market asset under Zora's current model. Do not buy our own posts merely to manufacture activity. Measure organic holders/trades and creator rewards.

### 2. Ko-fi

List digital wallpaper and printable bundles with clear personal-use/commercial-use license boundaries. Add a limited custom generative-art commission offer.

Suggested starting tests (USD):
- Phone wallpaper pack: $5–$8
- Desktop + phone bundle: $9–$14
- Printable art pack: $12–$20
- Creator asset pack: $19–$39
- Custom generative commission: $75–$150 depending on scope

### 3. itch.io

Package the generator itself as a creator/developer asset only if the repository license permits it and the package is documented. Also test themed asset packs with pay-what-you-want above a minimum.

### 4. OpenSea

Mint a limited NFT edition only after checking live network costs. Prefer a lower-cost supported chain such as Base for the pilot unless collector demand supports another chain. The owner's wallet must sign deployment/mint/listing transactions.

### 5. Rarible

Cross-distribute where technically and contractually appropriate. Consider a branded community marketplace only after the collection has traction; do not add infrastructure before demand exists.

### 6. Magic Eden

Use only for a deliberate Solana collection. Magic Eden's 2026 marketplace direction is Solana-focused, so do not build a separate Solana deployment until the pilot shows enough demand to justify it.

### 7. Etsy and Gumroad

Enable after product-market validation. Etsy adds per-listing cost and transaction/payment fees; Gumroad direct-sale fees are materially higher than Ko-fi for small digital-product transactions.

## Demand tests

For each product/channel record:
- impressions
- profile/store visits
- clicks
- saves/favorites
- buyers/holders
- gross revenue
- platform fee
- network/payment processing fee
- refunds
- net realized revenue
- conversion rate

Kill or revise offers with traffic but no conversion. Increase distribution effort only on channels that show evidence of buyer intent.

## Rights language

Default rule: purchasing a token or digital file does not transfer copyright unless a separate written license says so. Never claim exclusive rights for a design that is being sold simultaneously as a non-exclusive download.

## Security gates

- Never paste or commit a seed phrase/private key.
- Never let an autonomous process sign an irreversible wallet transaction.
- Never approve unlimited token allowances unless the exact need is understood.
- Verify marketplace domains before wallet connection.
- Start with a dedicated creator wallet rather than a wallet holding unrelated valuable assets.

## Success trigger

Positive revenue means cleared or on-chain realized creator revenue greater than total directly attributable platform/network costs for the same pilot. Revenue is not considered earned merely because a token's quoted market price increased.
